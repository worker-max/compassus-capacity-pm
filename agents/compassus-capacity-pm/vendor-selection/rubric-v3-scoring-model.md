# The Scoring Model — Vendor Scorecard v3.0

How the vendor rubric works. Questionnaire `form_version 2026-08-19`. **Read this before interpreting
any score in [`vendor-dossiers-2026-09.md`](./vendor-dossiers-2026-09.md).**

## The governing principle

> *"The questionnaire is the rubric. Read the return top to bottom and fill the sheet top to bottom."*

Every one of the 17 questionnaire questions has a row on the scoring tab, in form order. Each vendor
gets two columns: the mark, and a wide notes column for the justification and the claim-versus-evidence
read. Thirteen rows take a mark. Three raise a flag instead of moving the score. Five are intangibles
carrying no points at all.

**The section grades are the product. The total is a sort key so eleven vendors can be ordered — it is
not the decision.** This sentence is load-bearing and is what makes the shortlist exceptions legitimate.

Weights are declared changeable; every total and band recomputes from them. **HCHB integration and
Sophistication together carry 40 of 100** — the rubric is explicitly weighted toward "does it connect
to our record" and "how much of the work does it do."

## A1 — Home Care Home Base, one rung of six

| Points | Rung |
|---|---|
| 20 | Live — established customer base |
| 16 | Live — small customer base |
| 12 | Live — through a partner |
| 6 | In development — with a date |
| 2 | On the roadmap — no date |
| 0 | None, and no path to one |

Three rungs are live, *because live through a partner is still live.* **Anything not yet live shows as
Conditional on the band, whatever the total.**

## Section B — scope, three marks per arena, 0–4

`4 Most of it · 3 More than half · 2 About half · 1 A corner of it · 0 Nothing here`

Nine graded areas across three arenas:

- **Capacity** — workforce supply · availability & reach · the capacity math
- **Scheduling** — demand & matching · routing & the week · exceptions
- **Engagement** — before the visit · when plans change · across the care team

**Where Section C contradicts Section B, believe Section C.** Scheduling and Engagement have four
underlying areas apiece, so the two that belong together are paired rather than inventing a split the
one-pager cannot carry.

## Section C — sophistication, one mark, 0–4

The Read / Assist / Control language from the initiative workbook:

| Mark | Level |
|---|---|
| 4 | **Runs it** — decides across the whole picture, and re-decides when things change |
| 3 | **Recommends it** — works out the answer and proposes it; a person confirms |
| 2 | **Checks it** — applies rules and flags problems; a person still works it |
| 1 | **Shows it** — surfaces the information; a person does all the work |
| 0 | Not addressed |

Score what the product does, not how much the vendor wrote about it. *How* it does something is a demo
question, not a reason to mark down. **Note the asymmetry: a 4 is the top mark but sits above the Assist
boundary Compassus has set for assignment and coverage — scoring high here can mean overreach.**

## Section D — clinician fit, one mark, 0–4

`4 Strong fit · 3 Good fit · 2 Workable · 1 Poor fit · 0 Not answered`

**No descriptions, on purpose.** Read D1–D3 and give it your own read — we know how our clinicians work
and what they will accept. Reasoning goes in the notes column.

## Section E — partnership, one mark, 0–4

| Mark | Level |
|---|---|
| 4 | Open to equity or a stake in what we build, and set up to build it with us |
| 3 | Ready to build to our needs as a design partner; ownership not addressed |
| 2 | Will take our input, but they own the roadmap and the product |
| 1 | A standard customer relationship — we buy what already exists |
| 0 | Not answered |

Read all four E answers, not only E2. **A discount is a discount.**

## The three stop-checks — flags, not points

| Q | Criterion | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| A2 | Customers, scale and references | **STOP-CHECK** if one customer, or no references |
| A3 | Measured impact | **Watch** if claimed with no baseline or period |
| C6 | When your product is down | **STOP-CHECK** if no uptime figure or contractual commitment |

The rule as written: *"resolved before advancing, not traded against points."* A vendor with no
continuity commitment should be stopped and asked, not quietly docked a few points.

**In practice this rule was not enforced** — MedArrive advanced with two open, HCHB with one. See
[`rubric-review-and-process-findings.md`](./rubric-review-and-process-findings.md), finding 1.

## The five intangibles — no points, allowed to disagree

`Strong · Neutral · Concern`, each with a reason and the reader's initials.

1. **Home health fluency** — do these read like people who have stood in a branch? Unprompted
   vocabulary, problems they raise that we did not ask about.
2. **Candor about gaps** — did they say "we don't do that" anywhere? A vendor who claims everything has
   told us something.
3. **Who wrote this** — marketing, sales engineer, or someone who built it.
4. **Durability** — will they exist in three years, will this still be their main business, are we
   uncomfortably their largest customer?
5. **The room test** — would we want these people in our building for two years? **Left blank until
   after the demo, on purpose.**

> *"If this section never disagrees with the numbers, it is not doing anything."*

And the stated bias guard: *"Not credible can mean they were vague, or that they were unfamiliar. The
protection is that every read is written down with a reason, so someone can ask what specifically gave
you that."*

**Structural note:** home health fluency did the deciding work on at least one exclusion while carrying
zero points. See finding 2 in the review document.

## Bands

| Band | Total |
|---|---|
| Advance | 80–100 |
| Consider | 65–79 |
| Hold | 50–64 |
| Decline | < 50 |
| **Conditional** | Shown whenever the integration is not yet live, whatever the total |

## The three closing notes rows

Per vendor, one line each — *"this is what we go and ask"*:
**What stands out** · **What worries me** · **What to go and ask (the demo agenda)**.

These were filled sparsely. Where present they are quoted in the dossiers.
