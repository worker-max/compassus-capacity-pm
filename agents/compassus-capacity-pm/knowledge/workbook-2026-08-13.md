## Scoring semantics

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Constraint** | `Hard` = the visit fails if it is not honoured · `Soft` = optimise toward it, bend under pressure · `Structural` = a fixed fact of supply or geography · `Event` = a demand trigger · `Derived` = a computed value · `Config` = a policy-set constant |
| **MVP Req.** | Day-one must-have for the software: `Yes` / `Maybe` / `No`. Sets the scoring **Weight** — Yes = 3, Maybe = 1, No = 0 |
| **Automation Posture** | The boundary we set: **Control** (software may drive it) · **Assist** (software recommends, human decides) · **Read** (software may only display it) |
| **Gating** | `Constraint` is Hard or Structural **and** MVP is `Yes` — a knockout: a product that cannot do it should be disqualified however well it scores elsewhere |
| **Conflict Risk** | `Y` where a vendor's built-in way of working could contradict how Compassus operates |

> **Posture is a boundary, not a preference.** A product that scores well by *driving* a variable we
> marked `Read` is **overreaching**, and the `Functional Scorecard` counts that separately from the
> rating. See the 16-variable overreach on HCHB Smart Scheduling in
> [`business-case-and-kpis.md`](./business-case-and-kpis.md).
