The week, run as a cadence

Capacity is perishable. A slot left empty on Thursday earns nothing, so the week needs one fixed moment when protected slots release to the next tier. Wednesday noon is that moment here; set yours to your SOC lead time.

Slot yield bench

Two referrals, one SOC slot. Score them on contribution per skilled visit consumed, because RN and PT visits are the currency the branch runs out of. Rates other than the CMS base are placeholders: enter your contracts.

Model: per-period payers pay the period rate when visits per period meet the LUPA threshold, otherwise each visit at the CMS skilled-nursing per-visit rate ($176.96, CY2026). Per-visit payers pay rate × visits. Cost = skilled visits × skilled cost + assistant visits × assistant cost. The $193 skilled cost is the research file’s blended, fully allocated estimate; the assistant cost is an example. Admin drag from authorization work is not priced in, so managed-care results are optimistic.
