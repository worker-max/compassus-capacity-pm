# Vendor introduction email — draft

**Subject:** Compassus Home Health — capacity & scheduling platform, information request

---

Hello [Name],

Compassus is evaluating partners for a capacity and scheduling platform for our home health
business, and we would like to include [Company] in that process.

**What we are trying to do.** We are treating capacity management, scheduling and patient
engagement as one connected system rather than three separate tools. Capacity sets what a branch
can actually deliver; scheduling fills that envelope; patient engagement turns a schedule into
delivered visits. We are looking for a partner whose product reflects that whole, and we are
starting by understanding what exists in the market today.

**What is attached.**

1. **A one-page overview** of the capability we are looking for, organized into those three areas.
2. **Our current-state process maps** — how scheduling works in our operation today, including the
   parts that do not work well. We are sharing this so you can answer in context rather than in
   the abstract.
3. **A questionnaire.** It is mostly open questions. We are more interested in how you think about
   this problem than in a feature checklist, and a candid "we don't do that" is genuinely more
   useful to us than an overstated yes.

**What we are asking for.** Please return the completed questionnaire by
**Friday, September 4**. It should take a focused hour or two — there is no advantage in volume.

**How the process runs from here.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Friday, September 4** | Questionnaire responses due |
| **Week of September 8** | Virtual sessions with a selected group |
| **Week of September 14** | We confirm who we are inviting on-site |
| **Late September** | On-site working sessions in Nashville |

We will review every response. Following that review we will invite a subset to a virtual session
to get to know one another and go deeper, and from those we will invite a smaller group to spend
focused time with our team in Nashville.

If anything in the questionnaire is unclear, or you would like to talk it through before writing,
reply and we will find time. We would rather you ask than guess.

Thank you for your time — we are looking forward to reading your response.

Best regards,

**[Name]**
[Title], Compassus
[email] · [phone]

## Notes for Colin — not part of the email

- **September 7 is Labor Day.** Evan's sketch had virtual calls "the week of the 7th"; the email
  says *week of September 8* so we are not implying a holiday start. It leaves Tue–Fri, which is
  workable for a condensed round but tight if the group is larger than five or six. If it needs to
  breathe, pull the due date to **Wednesday, September 2** and keep Friday the 4th for triage.
- **September 4 is the Friday of Labor Day weekend.** Expect some requests for a Tuesday-the-8th
  extension. Worth deciding in advance whether that is granted automatically or on request.
- **Placeholders to fill:** recipient name, company, sender name/title/email/phone, and the return
  address in the questionnaire.
- **Attachments to send:** `Capacity-Scheduling-One-Pager.pdf`, `Detailed-Flow-Composite.pdf`
  (and/or `Primary-Flow-Map.pdf`), and the questionnaire. Recommend sending the questionnaire as an
  editable file — see `README.md` in this folder for the format question.
- **Deliberately not in the email:** any mention of the number of vendors contacted, our internal
  scoring, the full variable inventory, or the cut structure. The email says what happens next
  without signalling how many survive each stage.
