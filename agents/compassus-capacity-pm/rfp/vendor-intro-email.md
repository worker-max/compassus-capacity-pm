Hello [Name],

I am [Name], [Title] at Compassus. We are a national provider of home-based care — home health,
palliative care and hospice — and our work is built around caring for people where they live.

**What we are trying to do.** We are treating capacity, scheduling and engagement as one connected
system rather than three separate tools. Capacity is what a branch can genuinely deliver.

us than an overstated yes.

## Notes for Colin — not part of the email

**What this email deliberately does not do**

- **No process maps.** The earlier draft attached the current-state flow maps. They are the single
  most revealing thing we have — they show our sequencing, our failure points and where we think
  the leverage is. Sending them tells a vendor exactly what to sell back to us. Hold them for
  Nashville, where they become the working material rather than the pitch brief.
- **No self-diagnosis.** No reference to what is not working today. That is the same problem in one
  sentence.
- **No shape of the field.** Nothing about how many vendors were contacted, how many advance at
  each stage, or how we score. The timeline says what happens next without saying who survives it.
- **No pricing ask.** Asking in round one anchors the negotiation and pulls a sales answer instead
  of a product one. It is on the reserve list for the virtual calls.

**What it does give away, on purpose.** The three-part framing — capacity, scheduling, engagement —
and the one-page overview inside the workbook. That framing is what makes the answers comparable.
Without it, ten vendors answer ten different questions. It is general enough to describe the
problem and specific enough to sort the responses.

**Placeholders to fill:** sender name, title, email and phone; recipient name and company; and the
return address for the completed workbook.

**Attachment:** `Compassus-Vendor-Questionnaire.xlsx` — the vendor-facing file only. The MASTER
workbook carries the expanded coverage grid, the reserve questions and the leadership vetting list,
and does not leave our side.
