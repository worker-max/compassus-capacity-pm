How to read it

  • Further right is more capable, not automatically a better fit. For assignment and coverage, Compassus has set an Assist boundary: the system acts with approval. Past it, a product must let us keep approval switched on.
  • The components pull in different directions. Among these four, the product that automates most shows least on screen, and the two that show most leave more of the approvals to people.
  • Learning and fit decide how well it serves 80+ branches. Branch-level rules and learning from feedback need someone at Compassus to own and review them.
  • Decision-making mirrors the formal scorecard's sophistication mark. The other five rows are our read and are not part of the formal score.
