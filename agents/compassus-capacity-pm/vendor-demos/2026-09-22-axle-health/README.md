## Bottom line

**Lift the hold and invite to the on-site, conditional on a joint data plan.** Axle is home-health-native and showed the full flow live: work queues, care-team matching, day-of-week bulk-out inside fulfillment windows, route optimization with patient texts, call-out recovery, and zip-level capacity. Its only blocker is data freshness. It switched off its HCHB integration because HCHB can't reliably deliver within ~10–15 minutes, and Compassus runs at 3 hours. Our real-time intake platform covers new patients; routine orders still need a faster HCHB feed.
