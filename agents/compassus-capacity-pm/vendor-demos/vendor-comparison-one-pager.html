Capacity & Scheduling Vendor Demos: Comparison

Axle Health (Sep 22), CareStitch (Sep 23), VitalisCare (Sep 24)

Compassus Home Health
Internal, for discussion

Summary

  • Axle was the strongest product. It is built for home health and showed recommendations for clinician, day and time. Go-live depends on getting HCHB data to them faster than our current 3-hour refresh.
  • CareStitch has the best tools for clinicians and for coordinating coverage, but it does not recommend or automate anything yet, and its HCHB integration is not in production.
  • VitalisCare is a hospice product. Its HCHB integration is live, but authorization, episode/LUPA logic, therapy and patient communication would all need to be built.
