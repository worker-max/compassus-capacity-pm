Capacity & Scheduling Vendor Demos: Comparison

Arya (Sep 28), Axle Health (Sep 22), CareStitch (Sep 23), VitalisCare (Sep 24). Vendors in alphabetical order. Sources: demo calls and each vendor's RFP return.

Compassus Home Health
Internal, for discussion

What each product is

  • Arya: an agent that texts the patient, ranks clinicians with a suggested visit time, and writes back to HCHB after office approval. Clinicians use text and short links, no app. HCHB by Citrix screen automation.
  • Axle Health: a home health scheduling product that recommends clinician, day and time with the factors shown and plots the episode in visit windows. HCHB write-back built but switched off pending faster HCHB data.
  • CareStitch: a scheduler workspace with capacity by clinician and day, a staffing-request workflow and care-team chat. It filters eligibility; the scheduler decides. HCHB integration is a read-only beta.
  • VitalisCare: a scheduling product with a live HCHB integration, used today in hospice. It ranks providers and suggests better visit days. Authorization, episode/LUPA logic, therapy and patient messaging would need to be built.
