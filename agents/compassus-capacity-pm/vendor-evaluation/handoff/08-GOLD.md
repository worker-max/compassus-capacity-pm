# 08 · Gold catalogue

The unprompted specifics that mean someone has stood in a branch, told us something at their own
cost, or thought about our problem harder than we asked them to. Same schema as the red flags. Kind:
**Differentiator** (only some vendors will have it), **Fluency** (they know this business),
**Candour** (it cost them and they said it anyway).

Gold is provisional until the field pass. Anything on five or more briefs is moved to a commonplace
list and struck; it is still true, it is just not gold.

| ID | Pattern | Usually in | Why it matters to us | Sounds like | Kind | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G-01 | Unprompted domain vocabulary, used correctly | any | LUPA, 30-day periods, recert windows, SOC-capable, supervisory cadence, face-to-face, time to initial care, front-loading. | G-03 | A conflict or ownership rule for two-system sync, stated | A1 | The hard part of an integration answer, and the one that makes bi-directional believable | *"HCHB is authoritative for orders; we own assignment; on conflict HCHB wins and we re-plan"* | Differentiator | Show a conflict happening |
| G-04 | Impact with baseline, period and site count; a metric that did not move; the confound named | A3, D3 | Honest measurement is rarer than good measurement | *"mileage fell 14%, but they consolidated two branches that year, so we attribute roughly half"* | Candour | Can we see the underlying report |

| G-09 | Capacity distinguished from scheduling, unprompted | C1, E1 | Our central finding, arrived at independently | *"capacity is what you can absorb; scheduling is who goes where. Most tools only do the second"* | Fluency | Which one do you think we should fix first |
| G-10 | The readiness gauntlet named: auth holds, POA consent, coding, a held state with auto-release | C3 | Where our capacity is lost today, and the cheapest test of an insider | *"a held visit with a reason code, released when the auth posts, then offered a slot inside its window"* | Fluency | What happens when the window is about to expire and it is still held |
| G-11 | A measured time to fill, or an escalation ladder with times | C5 | A product, not a workflow diagram | *"median time to fill is four minutes; after twenty, per diem; after forty, the DCS"* | Differentiator | Show the ladder firing |

| G-14 | The clinician's own case for the change: what they see about themselves | D3 | *"The case for the change, made to the person it lands on"* | *"clinicians see their own drive time saved and continuity with their own patients"* | Differentiator | Show a clinician's screen |
| G-15 | A boundary with a principle behind it | E4 | A product mind | *"we chose not to build patient voice; the EMR should own the record of contact"* | Candour | Would you build it with us if we asked |
| G-16 | Re-plan distinguished from repair; the stability problem named | C4 | An optimiser that reshuffles everyone's week nightly is unusable, and only someone who has shipped one says so | *"a mid-week re-plan never moves a confirmed visit or a clinician's first stop"* | Differentiator | Show a mid-week change and what stayed put |
