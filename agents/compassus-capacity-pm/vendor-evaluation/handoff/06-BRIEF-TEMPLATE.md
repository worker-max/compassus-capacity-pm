# 06 · The highlight brief

One file per vendor, `<Vendor>-Highlights.md`, twelve hundred words at most. The first screen is for
the leader; the rest is for the PM. The five tags are fixed words in fixed order so a column of them
can be scanned.

Quotes are the vendor's own words only, inline, never paraphrased inside quotation marks. Anything
paraphrased carries no quotation marks. Every quote carries a question id.

## Rules the template enforces

- **Suggested marks on rule-shaped rows only.** The A1 rung is a checkbox against the six labels.
  The nine scope marks are element counts. Sophistication is the ladder. Partnership is the equity
  ladder. The three flags are trigger rules. Always phrased as *the evidence supports X because …; a
  Y would need …*.
- **No mark, ever, on Clinician fit or the five intangibles.** Facts and the tension only. The room
  test stays blank; it is filled after the demo, on purpose.
- **No total, band or rank.** You may state the mechanical consequence *not live, so the sheet will
  show Conditional*, because that is a rule and not a judgment.
- **A silence is a fact.** *Not answered* under FACT, a flag at the catalogue's severity, an ASK.
- **Three, not ten.** Three gold, three flags, three asks on the first screen. Everything else lives
  in the by-question section. The leader should finish the first screen in ninety seconds.
- **The notes cells use the workbook's voice.** See `09-CALIBRATION.md`. Short declaratives, the
  specific first, `CLAIM:` and `EVIDENCE:` as a pair, and a doubtful note ends with what to do.
