How we mark it

Home Care Home Base  A1

  • 25Live, reads and writes, several customers
  • 20Live, one customer or one-way
  • 12Live via a partner, flat file or screen scraping
  • 6In development, with a date
  • 2Roadmap, no date
  • 0None, and no path

Scope  Section B

  • 3Covers essentially all of it
  • 2Covers most of it
  • 1Covers some of it
  • 0Little or none

Sophistication  C1–C5, C7

  • 4Runs it — decides across the whole picture
  • 3Recommends it — proposes; a person confirms
  • 2Checks it — applies rules, flags problems
  • 1Shows it — surfaces the information only
  • 0Not addressed

Clinician fit  D1–D3

  • 4Clinician keeps real control, adoption shown with data
  • 3Clinician keeps real control, adoption claimed
  • 2System decides, clinician can override
  • 1System decides, clinician cannot override
  • 0Not answered

Partnership  E1–E4

  • 4Specific terms and a change-management story
  • 3One of the two
  • 2Willing, no structure offered
  • 1Standard commercial relationship
  • 0Not answered

Flags, bands

  • A2Customers, scale and references
  • A3Measured impact
  • C6What happens when their product is down

These three raise a flag rather than moving the score. A vendor can score well and still carry one. We resolve it before advancing rather than trading it against points.
