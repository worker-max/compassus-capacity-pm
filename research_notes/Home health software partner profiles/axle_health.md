## 2. History, Founding Story and Pivots

### Takeaway
Axle started in 2020 as a services company: a network of in-home clinicians that ran mostly COVID tests for telehealth partners, plus mobile phlebotomy. It grew that into a nationwide home-visit network, then in 2023 shut down services to sell its internal scheduling and dispatch tool as SaaS. This is a material pivot to understand.

### Cited Findings
- [C, secondary] Timeline:
  - 2020–21: an in-home testing network of phlebotomists, MAs, LVNs and RNs doing mostly COVID-19 tests for telehealth partners. The largest partner was Sameday Health.
  - Later: grew to its own home-visit network covering all 50 states, with revenue of "a couple million dollars".
  - 2023: shut down the services business and licensed its scheduling tooling to other providers.
  - The SaaS product passed $1M ARR within 12 months of launch.
  — [healthcarediscovery.ai](https://healthcarediscovery.ai/companies/axle-health/)
- [C] Job postings from 2021–22 describe Axle Health as "a modern health tech company seeking high quality Mobile Phlebotomist for in-home patient calls". Duties included venipuncture in homes and delivering specimens to labs. — [Soma Capital/DirectShifts posting](https://jobs.somacap.com/companies/directshifts/jobs/12748005-mobile-phlebotomist-in-home-patient-calls)
- [C] Simplify lists services "delivered by RNs, LPNs, medical assistants, and phlebotomists" and customers including "telehealth companies, health systems, payors, and researchers". This listing likely reflects the services era. — [Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/c/9871b511-33dc-45fd-b3f1-5ced0aab99e6)
- [C] A headline describes the shift as "Axle Health Pivots to SaaS Model and Secures $10 Million Series A". The page itself returned HTTP 410. — [AIM Research](https://aimresearch.co/?p=15805)
- Milestones:
  - 2020: founded. — [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/axle-health)
  - W21: Y Combinator. — [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/axle-health)
  - Feb 22–23, 2024: $4.2M seed. — [HLTH](https://hlth.com/insights/news/axle-health-raises-4-2m-to-expand-home-health-care-software-2024-02-23)
  - May 22, 2025: $10M Series A. — [Gunderson](https://www.gunder.com/en/news-insights/client-news/axle-health-announces-dollar10m-series-a-to-advance-home-health-operations)

### Inferences
- Axle's operating DNA comes from field dispatch: Uber-style routing applied to clinicians. Its product was built first for its own clinicians, which is a plus for usability in the field. Its home health clinical-workflow depth (OASIS, episode/LUPA visit-frequency rules, hospice IDG) is less proven.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the exact date the services business shut down or the exact SaaS launch date (the secondary source says 2023).

## 6. Products and Integrations

### Takeaway
Axle is a scheduling and dispatch layer that sits on top of the EMR. Its modules cover:
- AI scheduling and patient-clinician matching
- Route optimization
- Real-time visibility
- A clinician mobile app
- An office dashboard
- Patient engagement (SMS and voice ETAs and confirmations)
- Analytics
- Developer APIs

Integrations named include Epic, athenahealth, WellSky, Netsmart, HCHB and Statewise. Axxess, MatrixCare and KanTime are not named.

### Cited Findings
- [C] Website modules and features:
  - AI Scheduling: matches patients and clinicians, adapts to cancellations and delays, and supports office-led or clinician-led scheduling.
  - Route Optimization.
  - Real-Time Visibility: location, adherence, coverage gaps and re-routing.
  - Patient Engagement: reminders and live ETAs by SMS or phone, plus feedback and confirmations.
  - Developer APIs.
  - An "advanced scheduling module" designed to complement an existing EMR "without changing downstream workflows".
  — [axlehealth.com](https://www.axlehealth.com)
- [C] Integration logos and mentions on the website: Epic, athenahealth, WellSky, Statewise, Netsmart, and HCHB (named in a testimonial). — [axlehealth.com](https://www.axlehealth.com)
- [C] Earlier platform description: clinician mobile app, operations dashboard, proprietary logistics algorithms, and a patient engagement and booking solution. — [HLTH](https://hlth.com/insights/news/axle-health-raises-4-2m-to-expand-home-health-care-software-2024-02-23)
- [C] Scheduling inputs: traffic, provider credentials, patient needs and geography. Automated reminders and ETAs. — [HHCN](https://homehealthcarenews.com/2025/05/axle-health-raises-10m-to-accelerate-ai-home-based-care-tech-development/)
- [C] In the Cityblock deployment, visit times were generated from drive time, service duration, eligibility, territory coverage and availability. Clinicians still document in the EMR. Integration ran through Axle's API and "custom integration platform". — [Cityblock case study](https://www.axlehealth.com/cityblock-increases-clinician-productivity)
- [C, secondary] Customer types per Business Insider: large health systems, independent home health agencies, mobile phlebotomy providers, and high-acuity dispatch services. — [search summary of BI](https://homehealthcarenews.com/2025/05/axle-health-raises-10m-to-accelerate-ai-home-based-care-tech-development/)
- [C] Pricing is based on estimated customer savings from an ROI analysis. Each customer gets a dedicated implementation manager. An ROI calculator is offered. — [axlehealth.com](https://www.axlehealth.com)
- Past product: in-home testing and phlebotomy services, discontinued in 2023. — [healthcarediscovery.ai](https://healthcarediscovery.ai/companies/axle-health/)
- [C, secondary] Patent status is inconsistent: the website and a 2024 interview say "patented", while Series A materials say "patent-pending". — [healthcarediscovery.ai](https://healthcarediscovery.ai/companies/axle-health/)

### Inferences
- Axle is a complement to the EMR, not a replacement. For a hospice and home health operator, the key question is whether the HCHB integration (Compassus's likely EMR) is bidirectional and in production, or limited to a single customer.

### Gaps
- [NF] I found no evidence of Axxess, MatrixCare or KanTime integrations.
- [NF] I found no specific "AI agent", intake or referral-management module.
- [NF] I found no confirmation that the generative-AI and voice patient engagement promised in May 2025 shipped.
- [NF] I found no hospice-specific workflows (IDG, on-call, visit frequency by discipline).

## 10. Partnership Considerations for Compassus (Capacity / Scheduling)

### Takeaway
Axle fits a capacity and scheduling initiative well as an EMR-adjacent optimization layer: matching, routing, real-time re-routing and patient ETAs. It is also small, so Compassus could co-develop features such as hospice visit-frequency rules and on-call dispatch. Diligence should focus on integration depth with HCHB, references at scale, financial runway, and IP and data rights in any co-build.

### Cited Findings
- [C] Axle positions itself as complementing the EMR "without changing downstream workflows", with flexible APIs and a dedicated implementation manager. — [axlehealth.com](https://www.axlehealth.com)
- [C] HCHB appears in a website testimonial. — [axlehealth.com](https://www.axlehealth.com)
- [C] The company is actively hiring an interoperability engineer. — [YC jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/axle-health/jobs/0cZvDZx-senior-software-engineer-interoperability)
- [C] Savings-based (ROI) pricing. — [axlehealth.com](https://www.axlehealth.com)

### Inferences
- Diligence asks:
  1. Production HCHB customers and integration direction (read and write visits).
  2. Hospice references.
  3. SOC 2 report and BAA.
  4. Runway and whether new funding is planned for 2026.
  5. Ownership of co-developed IP and models.
  6. Scalability to thousands of clinicians.
  7. Validated outcome data.
  8. Status of the promised GenAI and voice engagement.
- Savings-based pricing could align incentives, but requires an agreed baseline methodology.

### Gaps
- No public information on enterprise pricing levels, SLAs, or uptime history. status.axlehealth.com exists but was not reviewed.
