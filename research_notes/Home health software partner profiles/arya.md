## Identity and name disambiguation

### Takeaway
The home-health "Arya" is **Arya Health**, which brands itself simply as "Arya" (aryahealth.ai; app at app.aryaworks.com). It is a New York City startup founded around 2022 and led by CEO and co-founder Kunal Sarda. It builds AI "digital agents" for back-office work at home health, home care, hospice and other post-acute providers. Only one home-health-software "Arya" exists. The most dangerous collision is that **"Arya Health, Incorporated" is the former legal name of Yuvo, Inc.**, an unrelated NYC Form D filer. Any contracting or legal diligence must pin down the correct entity.

## Products

### Takeaway
Arya sells "One Agent with eight skills": **Scheduling, Compliance, Talent, Onboarding, Intake, Payroll, Engagement and Front Desk**, plus a free **Emergency Response Agent**. These are non-clinical, administrative agents only. There is no clinical documentation, OASIS or coding product. The agents work alongside the EMR rather than replacing it.

### Cited Findings
- [C] The eight skills, and the segments served: home health, home care, hospice, SNF, senior living, private duty nursing and telehealth. — [Arya homepage](https://www.aryahealth.ai/)
- [C] What the main agents do. Scheduling matches caregivers to visits and fills gaps. Onboarding claims 50% faster time-to-first-staffing and 70% less administrative work. Compliance automates license tracking and deploys in under a week. Talent claims 60% less recruiting effort. Shared capabilities include a unified data graph, multimodal channels (text, voice, email), co-pilot or autopilot modes and audit trails. — [Arya blog](https://www.aryahealth.ai/blog/arya-series-a-ai-system-of-action-distributed-care)
- [C] The ERA (Jan 2026) handles calls and texts on a local-area-code number per location, triages and escalates to on-call staff, and sends AI summaries to a dashboard. It is free, and "does not access detailed medical histories." — [HomeCare Magazine](https://www.homecaremag.com/news/expected-outages-inspire-aryas-emergency-response-agent)
- [C] Integrations: the vendor says it reads and writes "EMRs, ATS, HRIS in near-real time" but **names no EMR vendor**. — [Arya homepage](https://www.aryahealth.ai/)
- [E] An aggregator lists these integrations: "CubHub" (patient, caregiver and scheduling updates; possibly a garbled name), Snowflake sync, webhooks, and Google Sheets and Calendar. It also lists the LLMs used (Google Gemini; earlier Anthropic Claude via AWS Bedrock) and Talent Agent subprocessors (Supabase, Cloudflare, Bland AI for voice, n8n). It notes "no verified bidirectional EHR connection." — [AI Health Index](https://aihealthindex.io/vendors/arya-health)

### Inferences
- The Intake and Front Desk skills are the closest fit for referral and SOC capacity use cases. Scheduling, Talent and Onboarding are the most proven.
- The use of low-code and third-party voice infrastructure (n8n, Bland AI), if accurate, matters for subprocessor and BAA review.

- [NF] Public API documentation, pricing amounts, hospice-specific workflows (IDG, HOPE, bereavement) and OASIS or clinical documentation features (none appear to exist).

### Inferences
