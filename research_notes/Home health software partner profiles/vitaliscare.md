## Products, AI features, and integrations

### Takeaway
There are seven apps in four "suites," positioned as AI layers that sit on top of the EMR. The only named EMR integration is **Homecare Homebase (HCHB)**, which matters for Compassus because it runs on HCHB. Integration with WellSky, Axxess, MatrixCare, KanTime, Netsmart or Consolo is not stated. No public API or developer docs were found.

### Cited Findings
- **[CONFIRMED]** Product lineup (as of the August 2026 release): — [Homepage](https://vitaliscare.ai); [Press release](https://vitaliscare.ai/vitalis-care-hospice-news-product-of-the-year-nominee/)
  - **Care Suite**
    - **Vantage** (EOL transition prediction). Flags patients likely to transition within 7–10 days and refreshes every 10 minutes. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** It claims 94% accuracy versus about 67% for "typical EMR tools" (unsourced), training on "50+ billion clinical signals," and a transparent rationale per flag. It also claims 18% fewer transitions without visits, 23% more EOL care time, and a 34% final-week visit surge. The page describes it as "fully integrated" with HCHB. — [Vantage](https://vitaliscare.ai/vantage/)
    - **Ray** (HOPE app). Tracks the HOPE timeline for each patient and flags missed SFVs and overdue HUVs. — [Homepage](https://vitaliscare.ai)
  - **Coordination Suite**
    - **Sync** (AI scheduling). Matches caregivers to patients by location and workload and preserves continuity. It generates weekly optimized schedules with mid-week reshuffles, and offers Smart Choice / Best Mapping / Manual modes, an AI-vs-original toggle, and IDG care team views. Humans approve each change. It references an "Original HCHB schedule view." **[VENDOR CLAIM]** 55–65% less drive time and 27.8% lower mileage on the homepage, but only 30% travel reduction reported by users on the Sync page, which is an internal inconsistency. — [Sync](https://vitaliscare.ai/sync/); [Homepage](https://vitaliscare.ai)
    - **Sync360** (field staff app). Visit tools, GPS mileage and hours records, and mileage fraud detection. — [Homepage](https://vitaliscare.ai); [Podcast](https://www.huschblackwell.com/newsandinsights/ai-in-action-exploring-how-ai-is-helping-hospices-do-things-in-new-ways)
  - **Compliance Suite**
    - **Align** (compliance). Scans documentation across the organization for gaps and timing issues. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** "prevents up to 90% of audit denials." — [Homepage](https://vitaliscare.ai)
    - **Guide** (documentation app). Bedside prompts, real-time flags and voice-to-text. — [Homepage](https://vitaliscare.ai)
  - **Collaboration Suite**
    - **Bridge** (room and board). A shared hospice–SNF dashboard with automatic reconciliation. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** payment turnaround drops from 30–60 days to 6–14 days, with 80% approved immediately. — [Homepage](https://vitaliscare.ai)
- **[CONFIRMED]** Positioning: every app "integrates with your EMR," and the EMR stays the system of record. Schedules are designed to appear in the existing EMR so workflows stay the same. — [Homepage](https://vitaliscare.ai); [Podcast](https://www.huschblackwell.com/newsandinsights/ai-in-action-exploring-how-ai-is-helping-hospices-do-things-in-new-ways)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The CEO's long-term vision is a unified platform that could "reimagine the EMR." — [Podcast](https://www.huschblackwell.com/newsandinsights/ai-in-action-exploring-how-ai-is-helping-hospices-do-things-in-new-ways)
- **[CONFIRMED]** Pricing is based on patient census, with custom quotes, bundles, no setup fees, and auto-renewing subscriptions. — [Homepage FAQ](https://vitaliscare.ai)
- **[ESTIMATE]** Capterra has a "Vantage by Vitalis" listing describing AI that forecasts transitions 7–10 days ahead, with a risk dashboard and alerts. The page returned 403, so review counts are unknown. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1107897/Vantage-by-Vitalis)
- Content marketing includes a glossary, toolkit, library and a quiz. — [HOPE glossary](https://vitaliscare.ai/glossary/hope-assessment/)

### Inferences
- **Inference:** HCHB is the only integration evidenced. Vantage is "fully integrated," Sync shows an HCHB schedule view, and the customer testimonial comes from an HCHB operations director. The product is effectively HCHB-first, which aligns well with Compassus's HCHB environment.
- **Inference:** Because the stack is hospice-only, home health use cases (OASIS, PDGM, home-health scheduling) are not covered today.

## Risks, red flags, and partnership considerations

### Takeaway
Vitalis Care looks like a fast-shipping, HCHB-native, hospice-specific AI vendor, which is a strong functional fit for Compassus. The main diligence risks are corporate opacity (an Israeli Ltd. under a studio structure, no disclosed funding, a single named executive), thin independent validation of its headline claims, and possible offshore PHI handling.

### Cited Findings
- **Positive fit signals:**
  - HCHB integration and an HCHB-operator customer. — [Vantage](https://vitaliscare.ai/vantage/); [Homepage](https://vitaliscare.ai)
  - A broad hospice-ops suite: EOL prediction, HOPE, scheduling, compliance, and SNF room-and-board. — [Homepage](https://vitaliscare.ai)
  - "EMR stays the system of record" positioning. — [Homepage](https://vitaliscare.ai)
  - Rapid shipping from one app to seven. — [Press release](https://vitaliscare.ai/vitalis-care-hospice-news-product-of-the-year-nominee/)
  - KPI-based implementations. — [Podcast](https://www.huschblackwell.com/newsandinsights/ai-in-action-exploring-how-ai-is-helping-hospices-do-things-in-new-ways)
- **Red flags and data gaps:**
  - No Form D filings and no disclosed investors. — [EDGAR](https://efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index?q=%22Vitalis%20Care%22&forms=D)
  - Headcount figures conflict across sources. — [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/c/integralytic/433681601); [Integralytic](https://integralytic.com/)
  - Unsourced performance claims, such as 94% accuracy and the "~67%" comparator. — [Vantage](https://vitaliscare.ai/vantage/)
  - Scheduling savings conflict between the homepage (55–65%) and the Sync page (30%). — [Sync](https://vitaliscare.ai/sync/)
  - The same person appears on multiple testimonials, and most testimonials are anonymous. — [Homepage](https://vitaliscare.ai)
  - The company's own accounts of which product came first are inconsistent. — [Press release](https://vitaliscare.ai/vitalis-care-hospice-news-product-of-the-year-nominee/)
  - No litigation, layoffs or negative reviews were found. This reflects a thin footprint, not a verified clean record.

### Inferences
- **Build-partner considerations for Compassus:**
  - Confirm the contracting entity and BAA signer. Clarify Israel-based access to PHI.
  - Request the SOC 2 Type II report.
  - Request Vantage validation data on Compassus's own population.
  - Confirm the HCHB integration method and whether Vitalis is an HCHB partner.
  - Clarify IP ownership for co-built products, given the studio model (Integralytic spins products into new startups).
  - Assess key-person risk around the CEO, who also leads other portfolio companies.
  - Check financial runway.
  - Note there is no home health product today.
